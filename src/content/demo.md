## How booking works

Fill in the short form at [/demo](/demo), three fields: name, work email, company. Then pick a slot on the calendar that opens next. Your details go to the founder, not a sales queue. Agents can request a demo programmatically by POSTing to `/api/inquiry` with `source: "demo"`.

## What the demo covers

- The app on your real messages: WhatsApp in one fast window, with the agent beside every chat.
- The privacy model: what the agent can read, what it never touches, and how drafts stay yours to send.
- Rollout and plans. Pricing is published at [/pricing](/pricing), so the call confirms fit, not the number.
