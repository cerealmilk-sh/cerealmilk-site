## What happens after you send this

Your message goes straight to Daniel, the founder of Cereal Milk. The first reply is a real person reading your brief, not an autoresponder: either a few clarifying questions or a suggested time to talk.

## What to include

One or two lines on each of these is enough for a useful first reply:

- **The team.** Solo or a firm, rough team size, and which CRM you run (Attio or Affinity).
- **Where your deals happen.** WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email, or all three.
- **What you want.** A demo, a pilot for the team, a question about the app, or something else.
