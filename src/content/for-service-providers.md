Cereal Milk for service providers: clients don't email you, they text you. Agencies, consultancies, recruiters, brokers: the work is sold in WhatsApp and kept alive in DMs. Cereal Milk puts every client channel in one keyboard-first desktop window, with an AI agent beside every chat, so the client, the referral, and the prospect are one keystroke apart.

## The whole engagement lives in one thread

The conversations that pay the firm are hashed out in chat. None of it reaches the record. The scope gets agreed at 11pm with three bullet points and a thumbs-up; six weeks later it is the whole disagreement. Your best client introduces your next one in a thread only you can see, so to the firm that pipeline does not exist. And the client who went quiet three weeks ago went unnoticed, because nothing was watching the channel where the relationship actually lives.

## One window. One record. No data entry.

- **Every channel a client moves through.** The official WhatsApp Web, LinkedIn, and Gmail, natively. The client, the referral, and the prospect are one keystroke apart.
- **Full context before you type.** Engagement, owner, next step, live beside the chat. Read and edit the record without leaving the conversation.
- **The paper trail writes itself.** The scope, the approval, the line that settles a dispute: filed to the right record in one keystroke, deduplicated and timestamped.

## Client confidences stay confidential

Every thread is born private and stays that way until you decide otherwise. You file the scope line; the venting about their board never moves. The privacy gate is enforced on the server, so a private message cannot sync by accident, by cron, or by bulk action. See [Security and privacy](/security).
