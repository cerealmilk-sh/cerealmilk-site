## Frequently asked questions

### Can I see it first?

Yes. [Book a demo](/demo) for 30 minutes on a screen-share with the founder, on your own pipeline, before you buy. Plans are monthly and cancel any month, so there is no lock-in either way.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes, and it needs no card. [Download the app](/download) and create your account: the 7-day free trial of the full product starts right there, on your own accounts. When it ends, nothing is charged until you pick a plan inside the app: Starter at $30 a month or Business at $40 per user.

### What is the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter is for one person. Business is for a team: everyone shares one CRM of record, team privacy controls are enforced server-side, and billing is per seat, so inviting a teammate just adds a seat. Checkout creates your team; guided setup and CRM mapping on a call are included.

### Monthly or yearly?

Either. Starter is $30 a month or $300 a year; Business is $40 a month or $396 a year. Yearly is about two months free, and monthly cancels any month.

### Any usage fees?

No. No per-contact fees, no per-message fees, no AI surcharges, and no WhatsApp platform fees, because the app runs on your own accounts.
