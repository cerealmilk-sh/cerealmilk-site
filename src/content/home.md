## The problem

Your best deals leave no record. Sourcing happens in DMs, diligence happens in group chats, LPs live on WhatsApp, and none of it reaches the CRM:

- The deck in the padel thread: read, replied to, never filed. Six weeks later it is forty scrolls up, and the founder has signed with someone who answered twice.
- The intro in a LinkedIn DM: the intro that converts lands where no integration sees it. To your CRM, the relationship does not exist.
- The terms agreed across three apps: half in WhatsApp, half in email, closed on a call. Someone rebuilds it from memory for the IC memo, or nobody does.

Multiply by every partner and every channel. That is the real pipeline: invisible, and gone with whoever leaves.

## Why Cereal Milk exists

Cereal Milk comes out of deal-ops engineering for 68 venture funds and firms. Every engagement hit the same wall: the pipeline in the CRM was fiction, because the real one lived in the partners' phones. Nobody had fixed it, because every existing answer fails a fund in its own way:

- WhatsApp has no CRM. There is no official integration; whatever reaches Attio or Affinity, a partner typed in by hand after the fact.
- The bolt-ons risk your number. "WhatsApp CRM" tools ride reverse-engineered APIs, and when WhatsApp notices it is your number that gets banned.
- The exporters own your data. Chat-export services copy your founder and LP conversations into their own cloud; the record exists, but it is theirs.

So we built the missing piece: the official clients in one native window, the CRM docked beside them, and a privacy gate enforced on the server. The fund gets the record without giving up its numbers, its data, or its partners' privacy.

Your WhatsApp is your life, not just your deal flow. Every thread is born private, new and imported alike, until you decide otherwise.
