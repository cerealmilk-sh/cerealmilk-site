## The problem

Your best deals leave no record and no leverage. Sourcing happens in DMs, diligence happens in group chats, clients live on WhatsApp, and none of it is reachable by the AI tools you use everywhere else:

- The deck in the padel thread: read, replied to, never followed up. Six weeks later it is forty scrolls up, and the founder has signed with someone who answered twice.
- The intro in a LinkedIn DM: it lands where no assistant sees it. To every AI tool you run, the relationship does not exist.
- The terms agreed across three apps: half in WhatsApp, half in email, closed on a call. Someone rebuilds it from memory, or nobody does.

Multiply by every channel. That is the real pipeline: invisible to your agents, and gone with whoever leaves.

## Why Cereal Milk exists

Cereal Milk comes out of deal-ops engineering for 68 venture funds and firms. Every engagement hit the same wall: the work lived in chat, and chat was the one surface no AI tool could touch. Every existing answer fails in its own way:

- WhatsApp has no official integration, so nothing your agents can reach ever sees it.
- The bolt-ons risk your number. "WhatsApp AI" tools ride reverse-engineered APIs, and when WhatsApp notices, it is your number that gets banned.
- The exporters own your data. Chat-export services copy your conversations into their own cloud; the context exists, but it is theirs.

So we built the missing piece: the official clients in one desktop window, an AI agent docked beside them running on your own model account, and one-click Markdown export of any thread. You get the leverage without giving up your number, your data, or your privacy.
