## Install on Mac (3 steps)

Cereal Milk is a direct download (not the Mac App Store). It's signed and notarized by Apple, so the first launch is one standard confirmation, then it's a normal double-click.

1. **Open the DMG.** Open the downloaded CerealMilk.dmg and drag Cereal Milk into your Applications folder.
2. **Approve the first launch.** Double-click Cereal Milk. macOS asks once to confirm an app downloaded from the internet; click Open.
3. **Sign in and connect.** Sign in with Google or email (free, no card), scan the WhatsApp QR with your phone, then connect your model: Claude and ChatGPT use the sign-in you already have, Gemini and custom endpoints take an API key.

## Install on Windows

1. **Run the installer.** Open the downloaded CerealMilk-Setup.exe. Windows may show a SmartScreen prompt for a new app; choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. **Sign in and connect.** Same as on Mac: sign in with Google or email (free, no card), scan the WhatsApp QR, connect your model account.
