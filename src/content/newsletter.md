The Cereal Milk Field Notes is one email when something ships from Cereal Milk: new releases, new capabilities, and field notes from the build. No spam, unsubscribe anytime.

## What a subscriber gets

Each issue is one of three things:

- A release note that matters: a new channel, a new CRM capability, a meaningful fix.
- A field note from building the product: what worked, what broke, what changed.
- Occasionally, something we published in [the open docs](/docs).

## If you prefer to read on the site

Everything the Field Notes announces also lands on this site and in the app's release notes.
