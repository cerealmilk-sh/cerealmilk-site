Cereal Milk brand concepts

Working concepts for CER-6 (logo) and CER-7 (clippy profile picture). Everything builds on the established pixel milk-bowl direction from the brand/milk-bowl-mark branch: 14x14 cell grid, flat cells, crisp edges, near-black tile, and the hand-drawn 5x7 bitmap alphabet.

Status: concepts for review, not final. Nothing in this folder ships. Winners get folded into scripts/generate-brand-assets.py and Logo.tsx.
